Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Controller

MyDetailer Ltd (registered in England & Wales) is the data controller for personal data collected through this platform. Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at: dpo@mydetailer.co.uk

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Car Owners: Name, email, phone number, postcode, vehicle details, and service preferences.
  • Business Subscribers: Business name, contact name, email, phone/WhatsApp, coverage area, business type, services offered, and payment details (processed securely by our payment provider).

3. How We Use Your Data

Personal data is used to:

  • Match car owners with local detailing professionals
  • Deliver quote requests and lead notifications
  • Process subscription payments and manage accounts
  • Send platform communications and service updates
  • Improve platform features and user experience

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process data under the following lawful bases as defined by UK GDPR:

  • Contract: To fulfil our service agreement with subscribers and match car owners with detailers.
  • Legitimate Interest: To improve platform quality, prevent fraud, and communicate relevant updates.
  • Consent: For optional marketing communications (you may withdraw consent at any time).

5. Data Sharing

We share personal data only with:

  • Matched detailing professionals (limited to name, vehicle details, and contact info for quote fulfilment)
  • Payment processors (Stripe or equivalent) for secure transaction handling
  • Infrastructure providers strictly necessary for platform operation

We never sell personal data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Business subscriber data is retained for the duration of the subscription plus 6 years for financial record-keeping obligations. You may request deletion at any time subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office)

To exercise any right, email: dpo@mydetailer.co.uk

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for platform functionality and optional analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
