UK Car Detailing Pricing Guide

Transparent average prices so you know what to expect. Costs vary by vehicle size, condition, and location.

Service Packages

Wash Only

From £20 – £40

Exterior hand wash, pressure rinse, wheel cleaning, and dry. A quick refresh for your vehicle's outer surfaces.

Factors affecting your price:

  • SUV vs Hatchback (size surcharge)
  • Excessive mud or road grime
  • Alloy wheel deep-clean add-on

Mini Valet

From £45 – £80

Quick wash, interior vacuum, dust wipe, and tire shine. Ideal for regular maintenance between full details.

Factors affecting your price:

  • Pet hair removal (extra time)
  • SUV / 7-seater vs Hatchback
  • Smoke odour elimination

Full Detail

From £150 – £350

Deep interior shampoo, leather treatment, multi-stage exterior polish, and wax seal. The complete rejuvenation package.

Factors affecting your price:

  • Heavy interior staining or pet hair
  • Leather vs fabric seat treatment
  • SUV or luxury vehicle size

Ceramic Coating

From £300 – £600

Professional-grade nano-ceramic protection providing years of hydrophobic shine and UV resistance. Usually includes paint correction prep.

Factors affecting your price:

  • Number of coating layers
  • Heavy swirl marks needing pre-correction
  • Product brand (Gtechniq vs entry-level)

Paint Correction

From £200 – £450

Machine polishing to remove swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation. Restores depth and clarity to your paintwork.

Factors affecting your price:

  • Single-stage vs multi-stage correction
  • Severity of swirl marks / scratches
  • Hard vs soft paint (varies by brand)

PPF / Paint Protection Film

From £800 – £2,000

Self-healing thermoplastic urethane film applied to high-impact areas. Protects against stone chips, scratches, and road debris.

Factors affecting your price:

  • Full body wrap vs front-end only
  • Film brand (XPEL, SunTek, 3M)
  • Complex panel shapes on supercars

Factors That Affect Your Price

Vehicle Size

Larger vehicles (SUVs, supercars) require more product, time, and panel coverage — expect 20–50% more.

Paint Condition

Heavy swirl marks or oxidation require extra correction stages, increasing both time and cost significantly.

Location

Urban areas like London or Manchester tend to be 10–30% higher than rural locations due to overheads.

Product & Brand Quality

Premium brands like Gtechniq Crystal Serum or XPEL Ultimate Plus command higher prices than entry-level options.
